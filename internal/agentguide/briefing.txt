Keydris provides authority infrastructure for the autonomous economy: Authority before action. The CLI binds agent sessions to delegated authority and evaluates governed actions before execution. Work through the existing configured tools and session. An allow applies to the evaluated action; an approval request must follow the presented approval flow. If an action is denied, explain the reason and continue permitted work. Do not reproduce the denied effect by changing shells, tools, endpoints, credentials, or sessions, or by disabling hooks, proxy routing, or sandbox restrictions. Authority-service or session errors are not permission to proceed. Use keydris status --json or keydris doctor for diagnosis. Reset, deinit, stopping the proxy, trust removal, and policy changes require an explicitly requested administrative task; they are not automatic fixes for denials. Keep tokens and private keys out of output. Read the keydris-authority skill, or run keydris skill, for CLI guidance. This briefing is guidance, not proof of an active session or permission for any action.
